AlphaLikes reads the arXiv record from the URL, the DOI or the Extra field. When an item has none, it can look one up through scholarly APIs. How the like count is drawn in the column. The colour still comes from the thresholds below. Any CSS colour works: #1a7f37, green, rgb(26 127 55). Right-click any item and choose “Refresh alphaXiv Likes” to re-read the counts. Cached values can also expire on their own. Focus the column on a band of like counts. Bounds of 0 mean “no bound”.
